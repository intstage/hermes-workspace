Hermes Workspace

Hermes Workspace is a private, single-user integration that connects the owner's local Hermes Agent on a Mac to Google Drive, Docs, and Sheets. It creates or edits documents and spreadsheets when the owner directs it to do so. It is not a public service or a sign-up page.

It uses the Google Drive drive.file permission for files created by the integration or explicitly made available to it. It does not request access to the owner's entire Drive, Gmail, or Calendar.
