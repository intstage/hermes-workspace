Hermes Workspace

Privacy policy

Effective November 24, 2026. This policy describes the owner's private Hermes Workspace Google integration, not the separate rclone-pool tool or the Hermes Agent project generally.

Access and purpose

With the owner's Google consent, the integration uses drive.file to access file identifiers, metadata, and contents only for files it creates or that are explicitly made available to it. It uses this data to read, create, or edit documents and spreadsheets for the owner's requested tasks, including passing a document or spreadsheet to the selected AI provider when needed to complete the request. It does not request Gmail, Calendar, or full-Drive access.

Storage and sharing

Google files remain in Google Drive unless the owner requests a copy or export. OAuth credentials are handled locally by the integration and are not sent to an AI provider or included in this website. Information retrieved for tasks and task results may appear in local files, local Hermes chat history, or backups configured by the owner. For tasks handled through an external AI provider, only the task-relevant data should be sent for the requested task; the provider's processing and retention depend on its service settings and terms. The integration is not intended to sell data, use it for advertising, or use Google Workspace data to train general-purpose AI models. Do not use Google Workspace data with a provider or setting that uses submitted data for such training.

Control and removal

The owner can revoke Google access under third-party connections in their Google Account, remove locally stored credentials and copies, and manage retention in their Hermes history and backups. Revoking access does not delete documents stored in Drive. This informational site does not ask for credentials or receive Google documents.

Contact

Use the support contact shown on the Google consent screen for questions about this integration.
